Creamy Garlic Pasta

Pasta is a type of food made from unleavened dough of wheat flour, water, and sometimes eggs, and is formed into various shapes. It is then cooked by boiling or baking, and often served with a sauce. Some popular types of pasta include spaghetti, penne, lasagne, ravioli, and linguine.

Ingredients

  • 2 teaspoons olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 3 cups chicken broth, or more as needed
  • ½ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ½ pound spaghetti
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • ¾ cup heavy cream
  • 1 ½ tablespoons dried parsley

Directions

  1. Step-1
    Gather all ingredients.
  2. Step-2
    Heat olive oil in a medium pan over medium heat. Add garlic and stir until fragrant, 1 to 2 minutes. Add butter and stir constantly until melted.
  3. Step-3
    Pour in 3 cups chicken broth; add pepper and salt. Bring to a boil. Add spaghetti and cook, stirring occasionally, until tender yet firm to the bite, about 12 minutes. Add more chicken broth if pasta starts to stick to the pan.
  4. Step-4
    dd Parmesan cheese, cream, and parsley and mix until thoroughly combined. Serve immediately.
